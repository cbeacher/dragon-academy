How to Play

Answering questions

Pick an answer for each question. A wrong answer never costs you anything — no score loss, no penalty — you'll just see the explanation and move on to the next question. So there's no reason not to take a guess.

XP and levelling up

Every correct answer earns XP, shown in the bar at the top. Once the bar fills, you level up — the egg in the middle of the screen moves one step further through its hatching sequence, and your level number goes up.

Hatching dragons

Once the egg finishes its hatching sequence, a brand-new dragon joins your collection and the egg resets to start again. There are 50 dragons in total, grouped into 10 families of 5 — the first dragon in a family is common, the last is rare, so it takes a while to complete a whole family. Tap View Collection any time to see who you've found so far.

Subjects

Use the picker at the top to choose what you're revising — Science (Chemistry, Physics) or HASS (Economics, Geography, History), or mix everything together. Some dragon families can only be hatched from a specific subject's questions, so switching subjects is the way to unlock every family. Your level and XP are shared across every subject — only the question pool changes.
